About

Julian Hernandez : Digital Illustrator, Graphic Designer and occasional writer. My work is interested in bridging the gap between visuals and stories through composition, visuals, and experimentation of different digital mediums. Heavily inspired by Artitst like Mike Mignolia and TB Choi use of dark inkly blacks with a combination of semi realistic anatomy and bright colors.
